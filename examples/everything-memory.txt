# everything-memory: The map, plus what is next to you in each direction, and where the key, door and goal are; what each move would do, written into its option; a question naming the next thing to reach: the key, then the door, then the goal.
# gen-10-01, after the moves east, north

You move one cell per turn: north (up), south (down), east (right) or west (left). Walls (#) block you, and walking into one wastes the move. Stepping onto the key (K) picks it up. A locked door (D) opens when you walk into it carrying the key, and you step through; without the key it blocks you like a wall. Reaching the goal (G) wins.

Map, with column numbers along the top and row numbers down the side (# = wall, . = floor, A = you, G = goal, K = key, D = locked door). A position (x, y) is column x, row y:

You are A at (7, 1). You are carrying nothing.
Objects: goal G at (1, 4); key K at (7, 2); locked door D at (1, 3).

North of you is a wall. South of you is the key. East of you is open floor. West of you is open floor.
The goal is 6 west and 3 south of you. The key is 1 south of you. The locked door is 6 west and 2 south of you.

Question: Your next target is the key K at (7, 2). Which move is the first step of the shortest path to it?

Options:
option_1: move south (down): you move to (7, 2) and pick up the key
option_2: move north (up): blocked, you stay at (7, 1)
option_3: move west (left): you move to (6, 1)
option_4: move east (right): you move to (8, 1)
